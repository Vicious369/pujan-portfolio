How I'll help

What this practice is being built toward. None of it is bookable today — see the roadmap above for what's actually in progress right now.

Cloud Configuration Audit

A structured review of an AWS, Azure, or GCP setup against recognized benchmarks — flagging exposed storage, weak network rules, and drift from best practice.

IAM & Access Hardening

Least-privilege access review, MFA enforcement, and credential hygiene cleanup — closing the access gaps behind most real-world breaches.

Security Posture Review

A right-sized risk assessment for teams without a dedicated security hire — plain-language findings, prioritized by actual impact.

Incident Response Retainer

On-call support for when something goes wrong — containment guidance and post-incident review, scoped for teams too small to staff this in-house.
